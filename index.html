    Subtitles

    Reads .srt, .vtt, .ass/.ssa and .sub. If the text looks garbled, change Encoding. Subtitles built into an MKV or MP4 can’t be shown by the browser directly, so the button above pulls them out with FFmpeg (downloads once, needs internet). Delay: G / H. Toggle: V.

    Sound

    Lip sync: minus plays the sound earlier (use this when Bluetooth headphones make the sound lag), plus plays it later. Keys J and K nudge by 50 ms. Works on videos; for a stream it needs the audio engine.

    Spectrum analyzer

    Volume above 100%, the equalizer and the analyzer need the audio to pass through the browser's audio engine, so they don't work on streams that block cross-origin access.

    Drag on the graph to shape the curve, or use the sliders. The shaded area is the live spectrum.
